AI-Powered Care Coordination

CareMait

A caregiver-first platform that combines scheduling, symptom tracking, shared communication, and guidance workflows for families and care teams.

01 Executive Summary

Problem

Family caregivers often juggle medication schedules, appointments, symptom notes, and communication across relatives and clinicians, resulting in burnout and fragmented care.

Solution

CareMait provides one unified workspace with smart reminders, care-plan timelines, collaborative updates, and AI-generated prompts to help caregivers make better day-to-day decisions.

03 Product Overview

Care Timeline

Daily plan with medications, appointments, and task ownership.

Health Journal

Track vitals, symptoms, notes, and detect trend changes early.

Family Hub

Share updates instantly with relatives and invited clinicians.

AI Copilot

Summarizes updates and suggests next-best caregiving actions.

Emergency Mode

One-tap medical profile export for urgent care scenarios.

Insights Dashboard

Flags adherence, sleep, mood, and symptom drift by week.

04 Monetization

  • Freemium: Basic reminders and care timeline for families.
  • Premium subscription: Advanced AI insights, exports, and multi-care recipient support.
  • B2B partnerships: Home-care agencies and clinics onboarding caregiver cohorts.
