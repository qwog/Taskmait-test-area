# Risk Register

1. **App Store rejection under UGC guideline 1.2** — Mitigated by pre-selected reasons only, custom EULA, rate limits, 24h takedown SLA, and obfuscated plate strings by default.
2. **Plate owner identification risk** — Mitigated by 5-tag minimum display threshold, obfuscated leaderboard format, score decay, aggregate-only badges.
3. **Coordinated brigade-tagging** — Mitigated by cooldowns, velocity detection, device-hash deduping, ban tooling.
4. **Reporting while driving** — Mitigated by motion modal, delayed tagging flow, and no quick-tag widgets.
5. **Privacy litigation risk** — Mitigated by no precise location collection, no photos in core gameplay, and zip-prefix city approximation only.
6. **Trademark conflict** — Mitigated by USPTO TESS search before launch and backup naming options.
7. **Tone mismatch risk** — Mitigated by copy review and exclusion of protected-class or identity-based language.
